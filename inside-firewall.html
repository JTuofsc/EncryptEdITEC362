Inside the Firewall

A firewall acts as a digital gatekeeper. It reviews network traffic and applies security rules to allow, deny, or log access.

Secure Sign In Emergency Lockdown
Key Terms
  • Inbound: traffic entering your network.
  • Outbound: traffic leaving your network.
  • Ports: service access points such as 80, 443, and 22.
  • Logging: saving events for security review.

Rule Types

Specific rules should come before broad ones.

Allow

Permits safe or required traffic such as HTTPS on port 443.

Deny

Blocks suspicious or unnecessary traffic to reduce risk.

Log

Records connection attempts for audits and investigations.

Mini Checklist

  • Use least privilege by default.
  • Only open the ports you truly need.
  • Log admin actions and sign-in attempts.
  • Review and revise rules regularly.

Lockdown Mode

During an incident, restrict traffic to essential services only, then restore access carefully once the issue is contained.
