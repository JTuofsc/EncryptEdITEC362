What is the difference between authentication and encryption?

Posted by Jamie • April 2026

I keep seeing both terms in cybersecurity lessons and I’m mixing them up. They seem related, but I know they are not the same thing. Can someone explain simply?

Authentication checks who you are. Encryption protects the data itself. One verifies identity, the other scrambles information.
